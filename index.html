Greedy Search ($T \to 0$)

Risk: Repetitive loops, local traps

📚 Algorithmic & Cognitive Insight

Factual Recall: The Capital of France

In factual questions with a single unambiguous answer, the output distribution has extremely low entropy. 'Paris' dominates with a massive logit score. Setting temperature low (e.g., T=0.1) yields reliable precision, whereas high temperature causes hallucinations.
